Medical Services

Medication Management

Ongoing, coordinated medication monitoring for mental health and addiction recovery — right here at the center.

Medication management is not a one-time event — it's an ongoing process of monitoring, adjusting, and coordinating the medications that support your mental health and recovery. Unlike an initial psychiatric evaluation, which focuses on assessment and diagnosis, medication management means having a licensed provider who stays actively involved in your care: reviewing how your medications are working, making adjustments based on your response, addressing side effects, and keeping your full clinical picture in view over time.

This service is designed for people managing depression, anxiety, PTSD, bipolar disorder, ADHD, or other psychiatric conditions — especially when those conditions are occurring alongside addiction. It's also a core component of care for clients on medication-assisted treatment (MAT) for opioid or alcohol use disorder. MAT medications like buprenorphine, naltrexone, or Vivitrol require consistent follow-up and clinical oversight to be effective, and that follow-up lives here, with a team that already knows your story.

At Barbell Saves, medication management is embedded directly in your overall treatment plan. Your prescribing provider is on-site and communicates regularly with your therapist and clinical team — so your medications and your counseling are working in the same direction, not being managed in separate silos. That coordination matters. Medications can accelerate progress in therapy, and therapy can reduce how much medication you ultimately need. Getting both aligned is how you actually move forward.

Most major insurance is accepted for medication management services, including AHCCCS, Banner Health, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, and Medicare. If you've been managing medications through a provider outside your treatment program and feel like those two parts of your care aren't talking to each other, that's exactly the problem this service is built to solve.

What to Expect

  • Initial medication review and evaluation
  • Ongoing monitoring and dosage adjustments
  • Coordination with your therapist and clinical team
  • Support for co-occurring mental health and addiction conditions
  • Same-site appointments — no referrals to outside providers
  • Most insurance accepted, including AHCCCS
