Commercial Insurance

UnitedHealthcare Coverage for Mental Health & Substance Use IOP

The Barbell Saves is in-network with UnitedHealthcare and Optum for adult intensive outpatient treatment in Phoenix.

UnitedHealthcare commercial plans — whether through an employer or purchased individually — are required under federal parity law to cover behavioral health and substance use treatment comparably to medical and surgical care. That includes Intensive Outpatient Programs (IOP) for adults managing mental health conditions, substance use disorders, or both.

The Barbell Saves Outpatient Center is in-network with UnitedHealthcare, which means lower out-of-pocket costs for you and no surprise billing for covered services. Many UnitedHealthcare plans administer behavioral health benefits through Optum, UnitedHealth Group's behavioral health division — if your insurance card lists Optum for mental health or substance use coverage, we verify it the exact same way.

Coverage details vary by plan — deductibles, copays, and session limits differ between plan types, and some require prior authorization before starting IOP. Rather than trying to decode your plan documents yourself, call us and we'll verify your specific UnitedHealthcare or Optum benefits for free, usually within the hour, before you commit to anything.
