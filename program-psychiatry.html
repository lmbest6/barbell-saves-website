Psychiatry

Psychiatric Visits

Medication management and psychiatric evaluation from licensed providers who understand recovery.

Co-occurring mental health conditions and substance use disorders are the rule, not the exception. The majority of people dealing with addiction are also managing depression, anxiety, trauma, PTSD, bipolar disorder, or ADHD — and attempting to treat one without addressing the other is a recipe for an incomplete recovery. Psychiatric care at Barbell Saves is designed to close that gap, with licensed psychiatric providers embedded directly in our treatment team.

A psychiatric evaluation at Barbell Saves is a thorough, unhurried assessment of your mental health history, current symptoms, medication history, and the ways your mental health and substance use may be interacting. Our providers are experienced with the complexity of co-occurring disorders — they understand that some symptoms that look like stand-alone psychiatric conditions are actually driven by substance use, and some substance use is driven by undiagnosed or undertreated psychiatric conditions. Getting that picture right is the foundation of a medication plan that actually works.

For clients already on psychiatric medications, we provide ongoing management: monitoring effectiveness, adjusting dosages, addressing side effects, and coordinating with your prescribing provider if you have one outside our center. For clients who are medication-naive or who have never received a psychiatric evaluation, we can conduct the initial assessment and develop a plan from scratch. Our psychiatric providers communicate regularly with your therapist and, where appropriate, your primary care provider — so your medications are always understood in context, not managed in a vacuum.

Psychiatric services are available to all ages and are integrated into our IOP, outpatient, and standalone service offerings. Many clients find that the combination of appropriate medication support and evidence-based therapy dramatically accelerates their progress in treatment. If you've been wondering whether a psychiatric evaluation might help, the answer is often yes — and we make it easy to find out.

What to Expect

  • Comprehensive initial psychiatric evaluation covering mental health history, current symptoms, and substance use interactions
  • Expertise in co-occurring conditions: depression, anxiety, PTSD, bipolar disorder, ADHD, and more
  • Ongoing medication management with regular follow-ups and dosage adjustments as needed
  • Full coordination with your therapist and primary care provider — your psychiatric care is part of your whole treatment picture
  • Available to all ages — adult and adolescent psychiatric care
  • Most major insurance accepted, including AHCCCS, Banner Health, Aetna, BCBS, and Medicare
Program Details

Services: Initial psychiatric evaluation, ongoing medication management, co-occurring disorder assessment, coordination with therapy team.

Conditions treated: Depression, anxiety, PTSD, bipolar disorder, ADHD, and other co-occurring conditions alongside addiction and behavioral health.
