Commercial Insurance

Aetna Coverage for Mental Health & Substance Use IOP

The Barbell Saves is in-network with Aetna for adult intensive outpatient treatment in Phoenix, AZ.

If you have an Aetna commercial plan — through your employer or purchased individually — your mental health and substance use benefits are almost certainly stronger than you think. Under the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, Aetna is required to cover behavioral health and substance use disorder treatment, including Intensive Outpatient Programs (IOP), comparably to how it covers medical and surgical care.

The Barbell Saves Outpatient Center is in-network with Aetna, which means lower out-of-pocket costs for you and no surprise billing. This includes Aetna PPO and HMO commercial plans, as well as Banner|Aetna — the joint Banner Health and Aetna plan common among Phoenix-area employers.

Coverage details vary by plan — deductibles, copays, and session limits differ between an Aetna Choice POS II plan and an Aetna HMO, for example. Some plans require prior authorization before starting IOP; others don't. Rather than trying to decode your plan documents yourself, call us and we'll verify your specific Aetna benefits for free, usually within the hour, before you commit to anything.
