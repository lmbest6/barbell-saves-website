Adults 18+

Adult Intensive Outpatient Program

Structured, evidence-based treatment that fits into your life — not the other way around.

The Barbell Saves Adult IOP is unlike any outpatient program in the Phoenix metro. We built our model around a simple belief: recovery isn't just about stopping a behavior — it's about building a life worth staying sober for. That's why every session integrates clinical treatment with physical wellness, community connection, and real-world skill building.

Our Intensive Outpatient Program runs Monday through Friday, with multiple session times available to fit your schedule. Each session is three hours long, which means you're getting meaningful, focused treatment without putting your job, family, or responsibilities on hold. Most clients complete the program in 90 to 120 days, though the pace is tailored to your individual progress.

What makes us different is the gym. Barbell Saves is built inside a fully equipped fitness facility, and movement is woven into every week of treatment. Whether you've never touched a barbell or you're a seasoned athlete, the gym becomes a tool for rebuilding confidence, managing stress, and experiencing what your body is capable of in recovery. Upon successful program completion, every graduate earns a free lifetime gym membership — because the community doesn't end when treatment does.

For those transitioning from a higher level of care — residential, detox, or a partial hospitalization program — we also offer step-down outpatient levels designed to support the transition back to daily life. Our clinical team will assess your needs and place you at the right level from day one. We accept new clients on a rolling basis, so you can join any week without waiting for a cohort to start.

What to Expect

  • Daily group therapy sessions facilitated by licensed clinicians covering coping skills, relapse prevention, and life skills
  • Weekly individual counseling sessions with your assigned therapist, tailored to your history and goals
  • Integrated fitness and yoga sessions — movement as medicine, not punishment
  • Wellness education covering nutrition, sleep, stress management, and the 8 Dimensions of Wellness
  • Peer support from staff with lived experience in recovery — people who have walked this road
  • Free lifetime gym membership upon program completion, keeping you connected to the community
Program Details

Who it's for: Adults 18+ dealing with substance use, addiction, behavioral health conditions, or co-occurring mental health and substance use disorders.

Schedule: Monday–Friday, multiple session times available. Each session is 3 hours.

Program length: 90–120 days. Step-down outpatient levels available.

Enrollment: Rolling — join any week, no cohort start date required.

Format: Co-ed, in-person, Phoenix, AZ (11042 N 24th Ave, Ste 102)

Insurance accepted: AHCCCS, Banner Health, Aetna, Optum, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Medicare, UHC Community Plan, and more.
