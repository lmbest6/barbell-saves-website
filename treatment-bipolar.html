Bipolar Disorder Treatment in Phoenix

Psychiatric care and outpatient treatment for bipolar disorder — licensed providers, medication management, and consistent support.

Bipolar disorder involves shifts between depressive episodes and periods of elevated mood, energy, or impulsivity (mania or hypomania). Getting an accurate diagnosis and consistent treatment makes a significant difference — untreated or misdiagnosed bipolar disorder can be destabilizing, and it's also one of the mental health conditions most commonly complicated by substance use.

The Barbell Saves Outpatient Center provides psychiatric care and outpatient treatment for bipolar disorder in Phoenix, AZ. You don't need a co-occurring substance use disorder to be treated here — we treat bipolar disorder on its own, as well as alongside substance use, which we address together rather than separately.

Signs of Bipolar Disorder

  • Depressive episodes — low mood, fatigue, loss of interest, hopelessness
  • Manic or hypomanic episodes — elevated mood, racing thoughts, decreased need for sleep
  • Increased impulsivity or risk-taking during high-energy periods
  • Irritability or agitation that feels out of proportion
  • Significant shifts in sleep patterns tied to mood changes
  • Difficulty maintaining stability in work, relationships, or daily routine

Our Approach to Bipolar Treatment

Because bipolar disorder is a biologically-rooted condition, psychiatric care and medication management are typically central to treatment — our licensed providers do a full evaluation and build a medication plan tailored to you, then monitor and adjust it over time. That's paired with individual and group therapy focused on mood tracking, routine and sleep stability, and building coping strategies for both depressive and manic phases.

If bipolar disorder is occurring alongside substance use, we treat both together through our Adult IOP — the two interact closely, and stabilizing one typically requires addressing the other at the same time.
